# Texture credits

The 2K planet and Sun maps in this folder (`2k_*.jpg`, `2k_saturn_ring_alpha.png`) are from
[Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), based on NASA mission data, and are licensed
under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

They are credited in the app on every celestial info card. Stars other than the Sun reuse `2k_sun.jpg`, tinted by
their temperature; Europa reuses `2k_moon.jpg`, tinted.
